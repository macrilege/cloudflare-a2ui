How is this A2UI?

AI understands your dinner preferences. The app sends A2UI messages for the restaurant card, party-size choices and time buttons.

Choosing a time sends an action. A2UI replaces the time slots with a review card, then a demo confirmation. Changing your plans clears the old time.
